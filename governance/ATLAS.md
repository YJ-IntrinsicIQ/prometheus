# Atlas

Single Source of Truth for Prometheus engineering sessions. Read this before development work.

## 1. Project Overview

Prometheus is a Python finance intelligence repository centered on the `knowledge/` package. It ingests company documents, creates company memory and business understanding artifacts, plans discovery questions, retrieves supporting chunks, and produces intelligence outputs.
It also defines a canonical Fundamentals Engine contract under `knowledge/financials` for financial statement normalization before downstream ratios, trends, CIM / PCIM, and investor analysis.

Atlas governs engineering work: architecture first, no duplicate modules, and governance updates when implementation changes architecture or module status.
The Prometheus Intelligence Manifesto in this folder is the governing product document for intelligence behavior, product explanation, and failure-learning standards.

## 3. Canonical Architecture

Canonical architecture, summarized:

1. Company/year context is created.
2. Raw documents are read and chunked by `scripts/smart_chunker.py`.
3. Company memory is built.
4. Business blueprint is interpreted.
5. Business classification is produced.
6. Discovery questions are planned.
7. `knowledge/retrieval` provides supporting chunks.
8. Discovery runtime executes module extraction.
9. CIM / PCIM intelligence artifacts are written.
10. `multi_year_memory` derives order-independent company-level history from yearly intelligence snapshots. A new deterministic company-memory commitments layer, `management_commitments`, now reads explicit yearly management promise evidence and writes `companies/<company>/company_memory/management_commitments/management_commitments.json`, `commitment_timeline.json`, `commitment_validation.json`, and `management_commitments_manifest.json`. It normalizes commitments across time, records announcement-to-evidence progression, and refuses unsupported delivery claims without introducing any new LLM call.
11. `intelligence/progression` is the shared progression kernel for evidence-first change tracking. It owns the canonical contracts (`ProgressionEvent`, `ProgressionState`, `ProgressionTimeline`, `TurningPoint`, `ProgressionSummary`), generic confidence and validation helpers, deterministic deduplication, and manifest metadata for progression streams. Domain interpretation remains outside the shared engine.
12. `company_memory/management_commitments` is the first consumer of `intelligence/progression`. It maps explicit management promises into progression events and state transitions, but keeps commitment-specific judgment, validation, and status semantics inside the management-commitments module.
13. `company_memory/projects` is the second consumer of `intelligence/progression`. It interprets bounded project evidence as execution progress, keeps economic impact separate from completion, and writes `companies/<company>/company_memory/projects/projects_registry.json`, `project_timelines.json`, `project_assessments.json`, `projects_validation.json`, and `projects_manifest.json` without introducing a new LLM call.
14. `company_memory/capacity` is the third consumer of `intelligence/progression`. It normalizes capacity-evolution evidence into canonical company memory, keeps funding deployment separate from operating utilization, and writes `companies/<company>/company_memory/capacity/capacity_registry.json`, `capacity_timelines.json`, `capacity_assessments.json`, `capacity_validation.json`, and `capacity_manifest.json` without introducing a new LLM call.
15. `company_memory/risks` is the fourth consumer of `intelligence/progression`. It normalizes business and financial risk evidence into canonical company memory, tracks risk progression and materiality, and writes `companies/<company>/company_memory/risks/risk_registry.json`, `risk_timelines.json`, `risk_assessments.json`, `risk_validation.json`, and `risk_manifest.json` without introducing a new LLM call.
16. `company_memory/management_commentary` is the fifth consumer of `intelligence/progression`. It normalizes recurring management narrative into canonical company memory, tracks how emphasis, confidence, and conviction evolve across years, and writes `companies/<company>/company_memory/management_commentary/commentary_themes.json`, `commentary_timelines.json`, `commentary_assessments.json`, `commentary_validation.json`, and `commentary_manifest.json` without introducing a new LLM call.
17. `company_memory/capital_allocation_outcomes` is the sixth consumer of `intelligence/progression`. It normalizes capital deployment outcomes into canonical company memory, tracks how capital deployment, follow-through, and later payoff evolve across years, and writes `companies/<company>/company_memory/capital_allocation_outcomes/capital_allocation_outcomes.json`, `capital_allocation_timelines.json`, `capital_allocation_assessments.json`, `capital_allocation_validation.json`, and `capital_allocation_manifest.json` without introducing a new LLM call.
18. `company_memory/management_quality` is the seventh consumer of `intelligence/progression` in spirit, but it is a synthesis layer rather than a progression adapter. It reads the longitudinal memory already produced by commitments, projects, capacity, risks, management commentary, capital-allocation outcomes, owner earnings, per-share compounding, financial truth, and management-related investor-panel evidence, then writes `companies/<company>/company_memory/management_quality/management_quality_summary.json`, `management_quality_dimensions.json`, `management_quality_evidence.json`, `management_quality_validation.json`, and `management_quality_manifest.json` without introducing a new LLM call or collapsing the result into a single opaque score.
19. Shared company-memory guardrails now sit in `knowledge/company_memory/guardrails.py`. Before a candidate can influence progression or synthesis, it must clear business-relevance classification, period resolution, and progression-materiality checks; items that are historical context, outside the analysis window, ambiguous, or semantically non-core are quarantined instead of being promoted into canonical memory.
19. `intelligence/investor_panel/company_memory_context.py` is the investor-panel longitudinal context adapter. It converts company-memory progression streams into compact doctrine-prioritized summaries so the analyst prompt can read change over time without dumping raw company-memory artifacts or turning the panel into a screener.
20. `apps/ask-intrinsiciq/src/lib/prometheus/ask-v2/` is the canonical Ask IntrinsicIQ v2 answer-routing layer. It routes questions by intent, prefers governed company-memory evidence before raw-document fallback, preserves the public answer contract (`Question -> Conclusion -> Progression -> Why it matters -> Evidence -> Raw numbers`), and keeps backward-compatible UI payloads without adding new LLM calls or company-specific hardcoding.
17. `knowledge/failure_learning` is the canonical dated incident registry for production failures. It separates observed facts from root-cause hypotheses, assigns stable category paths, and records durable safeguards plus regression requirements for later learning and review.
18. `knowledge/archetypes` provides the curated deterministic registry and pack vocabulary used to normalize multi-year themes, risks, metrics, and capital-allocation signals.
17. The Fundamentals Engine canonical path starts with aggregate year-level `financials`, which orchestrates `financial_discovery` -> `financial_extraction` -> `financial_normalization` -> `financial_validation` -> `financial_reconciliation` -> `financial_ratios` -> `financial_growth` -> `corporate_actions` -> `shareholding_pattern`, then writes `financial_audit_report.json`. A separate deterministic year-level `financial_basis_resolution` stage now resolves standalone/consolidated basis from generic document, discovery, and table evidence, writes `financial_basis_resolution.json`, and may safely enrich yearly normalized fundamentals plus existing truth-registry artifacts only when confidence is medium/high. A separate deterministic year-level `financial_truth_registry` stage now reads existing yearly financial artifacts and writes `financial_fact_registry.json`, `financial_truth_reconciliation_report.json`, and `financial_artifact_quarantine_report.json`, which together form the canonical financial fact registry / truth-reconciliation / invalid-artifact quarantine foundation for contradiction detection and downstream financial-readiness judgment. The `financial_quality` stage is dual-mode: at year scope it reads yearly financial artifacts and writes `companies/<company>/<year>/financials/financial_quality_summary.json`; at company-memory scope, `financial_memory` orchestrates `financial_trends` -> `financial_quality` -> `financial_attribution`, then deterministically derives the reusable multi-year financial memory set (`financial_year_index.json`, `financial_memory_manifest.json`, `financial_truth_pack.json`, `financial_trends.json`, `financial_quality_evolution.json`, `capital_allocation_financial_timeline.json`, `ownership_evolution.json`, `financial_memory_summary.json`, `financial_quality_summary.json`, `financial_driver_attribution.json`), and finally writes `financial_memory_audit_report.json`. Company-memory financial artifacts must now prioritize the year-level truth engine (`financial_fact_registry.json`, `financial_truth_reconciliation_report.json`, `financial_basis_resolution.json`, `financial_artifact_quarantine_report.json`) over degraded summary regeneration. A separate deterministic company-level `investor_financials` stage now builds investor-facing financial intelligence modules on top of the reconciled company-memory truth pack and writes `owner_earnings_bridge.json`, `capital_allocation_roi_ledger.json`, `working_capital_quality_drilldown.json`, `order_revenue_cash_conversion_tracker.json`, `per_share_compounding_analysis.json`, and `investor_financial_modules_manifest.json` under `companies/<company>/company_memory/financials/investor_financial_modules/`. These modules must remain deterministic, generic, and truth-pack-backed; they may not read stale broad warning text as if it were primary evidence and may not call an LLM. A separate deterministic `financial_pcim_validation` stage now validates year-specific financial PCIM safety against those audited artifacts, and company-level `audit` also writes a `financial_quality_scorecard` before Investor Panel reasoning is treated as financially safe.
16. Committee synthesis now combines the five analyst outputs into a canonical `committee_synthesis.json` through a deterministic skeleton-first architecture. Committee synthesis v2 adds a deterministic evidence-and-progression overlay that preserves committee brief compatibility while surfacing shared convictions, disagreements, turning points, judgment blocks, and evidence confidence without adding a new LLM call. The pipeline owns metadata, analyst coverage, canonical enums, financial warning manifests, and registry-grounded `critical_unknowns`, while the LLM is limited to narrative fill such as executive summary and watch-list prose. The committee brief / QA stages then render and verify that combined story plus financial judgment without reading raw documents directly. The v2 contract is documented in `docs/architecture/COMMITTEE_SYNTHESIS_V2.md`.
17. Fundamentals acceptance QA now sits above the audited fundamentals path and writes a final company-level readiness judgment under `companies/<company>/audit/` before the fundamentals engine is treated as trusted for investor analysis.
18. Ask IntrinsicIQ now also has a deterministic company-level backend output layer under `intelligence/ask_intrinsiciq/`. Its explicit `ask_intrinsiciq` stage reads existing company-memory sources when available, introduces no new LLM call, and writes only under `companies/<company>/company_memory/ask_intrinsiciq/`.

Artifact roles:

- Cleaned project status: when a valid sourced project has no explicit lifecycle status, the project cleaner must preserve it with `status = "UNKNOWN"` and an uncertainty reason. It must not infer planned, ongoing, completed, or commissioned status from mere mention of a facility or capability.

- CIM / PCIM: canonical intelligence contract.
- `company_memory`: transient runtime artifact.
- `company_memory/management_commitments`: deterministic history layer for management commitments, promises, updates, delivery judgments, and manifest-tracked source coverage across time. It is canonical company-memory intelligence, not a scorecard.
- `company_memory/projects`: deterministic history layer for bounded projects, progression, execution status, impact separation, and manifest-tracked source coverage across time. It is canonical company-memory intelligence, not a dashboard.
- `company_memory/capacity`: deterministic history layer for capacity evolution, utilization evidence, funding-vs-operations separation, and manifest-tracked source coverage across time. It is canonical company-memory intelligence, not a performance scoreboard.
- `company_memory/risks`: deterministic history layer for business and financial risk evolution, progression tracking, materiality assessment, and manifest-tracked source coverage across time. It is canonical company-memory intelligence, not a risk scorecard.
- `company_memory/management_commentary`: deterministic history layer for recurring management narrative, emphasis shifts, conviction changes, and manifest-tracked source coverage across time. It is canonical company-memory intelligence, not a scorecard.
- `company_memory/capital_allocation_outcomes`: deterministic history layer for capital deployment outcomes, later payoff, cross-stream links, and manifest-tracked source coverage across time. It is canonical company-memory intelligence, not a scorecard.
- `company_memory/management_quality`: deterministic synthesis layer for longitudinal management quality judgment across commitments, execution, capacity, risks, candor, strategy, capital allocation, owner alignment, and evidence confidence. It is canonical company-memory intelligence, not a scorecard.
- `intelligence/progression`: shared progression kernel for evidence-first change tracking. It standardizes event ordering, state transitions, confidence, turning points, and validation for multiple streams while leaving domain judgment outside the engine.
- `business_blueprint`: reasoning artifact.
- `business_classification`: classification artifact.
- `knowledge/archetypes`: curated archetype-pack registry activated by Business DNA for deterministic normalization.
- `knowledge/financials`: canonical fundamentals schema, unit-normalization, validation, corporate-action, and ownership-tracking contract.
- Financial monetary normalization: canonical normalized unit is INR crore (`value_crore`), while original reported value/unit must always be preserved.
- Financial schema rule: no LLM-calculated ratios or derived math belongs in the schema layer.
- Financial-memory eligibility is company-generic and year-complete: before building `financial_year_index.json`, the financial-memory stage must deterministically refresh `financial_quality_summary.json` for every discovered fiscal-year directory that has normalized fundamentals, then build the separate company-memory quality summary. A missing historical year-level quality artifact must not silently reduce a multi-year company to one usable year when the underlying validated financial artifacts are present.
- Investor-panel freshness is fail-closed: saved PCIM must not be used when governed `financial_truth_pack.json` or `investor_financial_modules_manifest.json` is newer than `pcim_v1.json`; CIM/PCIM must be rebuilt first. Analyst financial prompt compaction must order dated financial buckets and current metrics latest-year-first, while retaining older periods as trend context, and series-valued metrics must resolve their latest point by fiscal-year value rather than source-list position.
- Corporate-action rule: share-count-sensitive events must be captured as deterministic adjustment metadata, and historical EPS / per-share growth must not be silently restated in this layer.

## 9. Active Decisions

- `pipelines/run_company_pipeline.py` is the canonical production pipeline.
- `run_business_pipeline.py` is experimental; validated behavior should merge into the canonical pipeline.
- `scripts/smart_chunker.py` is the canonical chunker; `scripts/chunker.py` is deprecated.
- `knowledge/retrieval` is canonical retrieval; embedding/RAG scripts are experimental/manual tools only.
- CIM / PCIM is the canonical intelligence contract.
- AI prompts and AI output schemas require versioning; breaking changes require version bumps.
- `knowledge/financials` is the canonical fundamentals schema subsystem, and its yearly plus company-level artifacts now feed CIM / PCIM directly.
- Financial monetary values must preserve original reported value/unit and normalize to `value_crore` in INR.
- Financial schema / validator layers must not contain LLM-calculated ratios or downstream analytical math.
- `financial_discovery` is the canonical non-LLM stage for locating likely financial statement and note sections before any financial number extraction.
- `financial_discovery` items must carry explicit basis metadata (`basis`, `basis_confidence`) whenever standalone / consolidated evidence is detectable so downstream stages do not need to re-guess basis from raw text alone.
- `financial_discovery` must classify primary statements, explicit notes, accounting-policy text, auditor-report text, management-discussion financial summaries, and irrelevant financial narrative separately so downstream extraction can favor precision over broad recall.
- `financial_extraction` is the canonical non-LLM stage for capturing raw financial table rows with preserved labels, raw values, basis hints, typed values (`value_type`, `raw_number`), and `value_crore` normalization only where the row is genuinely monetary.
- Financial unit resolution must treat an explicit table-wide monetary declaration (for example, “all figures are in INR crores unless specifically stated otherwise”) as the default even when the same table contains percentage cells. Explicit `%` value tokens remain row/value-level percentage overrides; their presence must not retype unrelated monetary rows or erase their crore conversion.
- `financial_extraction` must also emit `financial_extraction_rejections.json` so rejected table candidates remain auditable instead of disappearing silently.
- The failure-learning registry now tracks both root-cause status and remediation status. Guardrails that were added in response to an incident should be reflected there so future runs can tell the difference between a documented failure and an implemented safeguard.
- `financial_normalization` is the canonical non-LLM stage for mapping raw financial rows into field-level normalized fundamentals, preserving source lineage, basis, original reported values, and unmapped rows.
- `normalized_fundamentals.json` is the canonical preferred-basis surface for downstream math. It must expose `preferred_basis`, `basis_options_available`, `selected_basis_reason`, `basis_confidence`, and `basis_manifest`, must preserve non-preferred evidence under `basis_views`, and must not silently promote standalone values into a consolidated preferred view or vice versa.
- `financial_normalization` must prefer primary statements over note summaries or management-discussion financial summaries when multiple candidate rows compete for the same canonical field.
- `cash_flow.capex` is a guarded normalized field: it should only be populated from clear investing cash-flow capex evidence or equally explicit capex/additions evidence, must preserve cash-flow sign semantics, and should carry `capex_abs_crore` plus `sign_convention` metadata so downstream FCF remains deterministic. Missing capex is acceptable as a warning; incorrect capex sourcing is not.
- `financial_validation` is the canonical non-LLM readiness gate that checks normalized fundamentals for required fields, consistency, unit/source integrity, and suspicious sign conventions before ratios are allowed downstream.
- `financial_reconciliation` is the canonical non-LLM gate between `financial_validation` and downstream financial math. It verifies that normalized fundamentals still point to relevant source lines, preserve the correct value type for EPS/share-count fields, and do not rely on unrelated or disallowed source sections before ratios or growth proceed.
- `cash_flow.fcf` should normally be derived from CFO plus capex provenance rather than loosely extracted from noisy text. Missing FCF because capex is genuinely unavailable is a warning path; populated FCF from an unrelated heading, balance row, or non-capex source is a hard failure.
- `financial_ratios` is the canonical non-LLM ratio-calculation stage that reads reconciled normalized fundamentals, preserves formula plus input provenance, emits null-plus-warning for missing inputs, and refuses to calculate on top of failed reconciliation for critical inputs.
- `financial_ratios` and `financial_growth` must carry explicit basis context (`basis_used`, `basis_confidence`, `basis_warnings`) so downstream consumers can distinguish a clean consolidated/standalone run from an explicitly unknown or warning-level basis state.
- `financial_quality` is the canonical non-LLM diagnostic stage with two deterministic modes. Year mode reads `normalized_fundamentals.json`, `financial_reconciliation_report.json`, `financial_validation_report.json`, `financial_ratios.json`, `financial_growth.json`, `corporate_actions.json`, and optional `shareholding_pattern.json`, then writes a year-level `financial_quality_summary.json` with section-level assessments, evidence metrics, warnings, limitations, red flags, missing-data notes, and investor questions. Company-memory mode reads `financial_trends.json`, converts multi-year growth, margin, return, cash-conversion, leverage, working-capital, ownership, and corporate-action signals into investor-readable positives, red flags, missing-data notes, and warning-aware quality sections, and writes the company-memory `financial_quality_summary.json` without valuation or recommendation language.
- `financial_attribution` is the canonical non-LLM company-level attribution stage that reads `financial_trends.json`, `financial_quality_summary.json`, and available multi-year company-memory artifacts, then links major financial movements to possible business events or management actions without overclaiming causation, and writes `financial_driver_attribution.json`.
- `corporate_actions` is the canonical non-LLM metadata stage for dividends, split/bonus/dilution events, face-value changes, and share-count comparability warnings, using normalized fundamentals plus raw financial table evidence without silently restating historical per-share values.
- `corporate_actions` must stay conservative: dividend cash outflow and dividend-per-share are separate signals, share counts must never be stored as `amount_crore`, QIP/rights/preferential issue rows must distinguish issue events from proceeds-utilization rows where the source makes that distinction visible, and rejected or nullified action-like candidates must remain auditable in `corporate_action_rejections.json` instead of being silently accepted or dropped.
- `financial_growth` is the canonical non-LLM growth-calculation stage that reads reconciled normalized fundamentals plus available ratio history, computes YoY and multi-year growth where evidence exists, records null-plus-warning when history is missing or unusable, and may derive margin changes directly from normalized fundamentals when ratio history is unavailable.
- `shareholding_pattern` is the canonical non-LLM ownership-tracking stage that reads normalized fundamentals plus available raw shareholding rows, records ownership categories and change signals only from explicit shareholding evidence, emits warning-only artifacts when real reports do not yet yield usable ownership rows, and preserves rejected ownership candidates in `shareholding_rejections.json` for auditability.
- `financial_trends` is the canonical non-LLM company-level aggregation stage that reads yearly financial artifacts, preserves year-level provenance plus comparability warnings, and builds the reusable multi-year financial trend view before downstream CIM / PCIM interpretation.
- `financials` is the canonical aggregate year-level fundamentals stage. It must run the deterministic yearly fundamentals stages in order, must still emit `financial_audit_report.json` when a downstream readiness gate fails, and must not bypass validation in order to produce ratios or growth.
- `financial_memory` is the canonical aggregate company-level fundamentals stage. It must run `financial_trends`, `financial_quality`, and `financial_attribution` in order, then derive `financial_year_index`, `financial_quality_evolution`, `capital_allocation_financial_timeline`, `ownership_evolution`, and `financial_memory_summary` before emitting `financial_memory_audit_report.json` for downstream CIM / PCIM consumption.
- `financial_memory` now also owns `financial_memory_manifest.json` and `financial_truth_pack.json`. These are the canonical machine-readable company-memory surfaces for year coverage, truth-registry readiness, blocked-vs-allowed downstream warnings, precise missing metrics, unreliable metrics, invalid/quarantined metrics, and investor-relevant follow-up questions. Company-memory financial summaries must not override year-level direct or derived truth facts.
- `financial_truth_pack.json` is now also the canonical hydration boundary between deterministic financial memory and downstream panel reasoning. It must record `source_files_checked`, `source_files_used`, `source_files_missing`, `usable_current_metrics`, `usable_derived_metrics`, `partial_metrics`, `derived_not_explicitly_reported`, `trend_durability_limits`, `precision_limits`, warning-policy buckets (`financial_warnings_allowed_downstream`, `financial_warnings_blocked_downstream`, `financial_warnings_rewritten`), and panel-readiness fields (`financial_panel_status`, `financial_panel_status_reason`, `financial_panel_usable_domains`, `financial_panel_limited_domains`, `financial_panel_blocked_domains`, `source_provenance`). If investor financial modules exist but hydration fails to populate the truth-pack sections they are responsible for, the truth pack must mark `financial_panel_status = invalid` rather than silently degrading to broad missing-data claims.
- `investor_financials` is the canonical deterministic investor-facing financial module stage built on company-memory financial truth. It must write `owner_earnings_bridge.json`, `capital_allocation_roi_ledger.json`, `working_capital_quality_drilldown.json`, `order_revenue_cash_conversion_tracker.json`, `per_share_compounding_analysis.json`, and `investor_financial_modules_manifest.json` under `companies/<company>/company_memory/financials/investor_financial_modules/`. Those modules may use reconciled truth facts, truth-pack warning policy, trends, quality summary, driver attribution, and corporate-action/shareholding metadata, but they must not use raw `source_chunk` text, stale broad missing-data wording, or LLM synthesis.

If a numerator is in INR crore and the denominator is an absolute share count, the only valid generic conversion is `value_crore * 10000000 / shares`. Directly reported per-share metrics such as EPS, dividend per share, and book value per share remain `reported_directly` and must not be re-converted as if they were crore values. `financial_truth_pack.json` now records `unit_validation_warnings`, `unit_validation_failures`, and `recomputed_metrics` so downstream consumers can distinguish repaired closing-share estimates from quarantined unit bugs.

When a derived per-share metric lacks either numerator or denominator inputs, `financial_truth_pack.json` must not place it into `usable_current_metrics` or mark it `present_derived`; instead it must classify the row as `precise_missing` or `partial` with `missing_inputs`, and it may emit the closing-share precision warning only for rows that were actually calculated from closing shares.

The resolver priority is weighted-average basic shares first, weighted-average diluted shares when a diluted basis is explicitly required, then closing shares as a fallback with a precision warning. If numerator and resolved share count both exist, the metric should be calculated; if either side is truly absent, it must be classified as missing or partial rather than emitted as a misleading fake usable row.
- `financial_pcim_validation` is the canonical year-level deterministic safety gate for financial PCIM. It must validate existing PCIM output against audited year-level financial artifacts, must not call LLMs, must not calculate new ratios, and must fail on raw leakage, invented financial values, hidden hard failures, or forbidden valuation/recommendation language.

It must stay deterministic, must not call LLMs, must treat missing revenue/PAT, failed financial validation, missing normalized INR-crore values, missing core traceability, or missing PCIM financial manifest as hard failures, and must keep `ready_for_valuation` false at this stage even when `ready_for_panel` becomes true.

It must expose named sections for `core_fundamentals`, `profitability`, `growth`, `return_on_capital`, `cash_conversion`, `balance_sheet`, `working_capital`, `per_share`, `corporate_actions`, `shareholding`, `financial_quality_summary`, plus `basis_used`, `basis_confidence`, `warnings`, `limitations`, and `source_manifest`.

It must preserve source-year and source-artifact traceability, exclude `source_chunk`, and carry `pcim_source_manifest` financial status (`financial_artifacts_used`, `financial_years_covered`, `financial_status`, `financial_warnings`).
- The canonical compact financial PCIM sections are `financial_fundamentals_inputs`, `financial_trend_inputs`, `financial_growth_inputs`, `profitability_inputs`, `cash_conversion_inputs`, `return_on_capital_inputs`, `balance_sheet_strength_inputs`, `working_capital_inputs`, `per_share_inputs`, `corporate_action_inputs`, `ownership_inputs`, `financial_quality_inputs`, `financial_driver_inputs`, and `multi_year_financial_inputs`. These sections must stay compact, deterministic, raw-table-free, and panel-safe.
- `multi_year_financial_inputs` is the canonical panel-facing summary of company-level financial memory. It must be derived only from audited year-level financial artifacts and company-level financial memory files, must stay compact, must exclude raw tables and `source_chunk`, and should be the preferred multi-year financial context surface for downstream investor-panel reasoning when present.
- `financial_quality_scorecard` is the canonical deterministic company-level financial readiness scorecard. It must aggregate year-level financial PCIM validation, audited yearly financial artifacts, multi-year financial memory, and panel-readiness signals into dimension scores and recommended next fixes, but it must not perform new financial interpretation or valuation.
- `financial_source_manifest` and `financial_panel_ready` are required top-level PCIM financial contract fields whenever financial PCIM sections are present. `financial_panel_ready` may be true only when usable financial quality evidence exists and manifest status is `pass` or `warning`.

- The financial truth registry is the canonical generic classification layer for capex, FCF, working-capital, debt, and share-count readiness. It may derive FCF, payable-days, cash-conversion-cycle, net debt, and capex sub-buckets when deterministic inputs exist; it must preserve provenance and warning normalization, and it must prefer `partial` or `unreliable` over broad `missing` when usable but limited evidence exists.

The preferred upstream surfaces are `financial_truth_inputs`, `financial_snapshot_inputs`, `financial_quality_inputs`, `owner_earnings_readiness_inputs`, `working_capital_quality_inputs`, `capital_allocation_financial_inputs`, `per_share_compounding_inputs`, `unreliable_financial_inputs`, `invalid_or_quarantined_financial_inputs`, `precise_missing_financial_inputs`, `financial_warning_policy`, `financial_panel_status`, `financial_panel_usable_domains`, `financial_panel_limited_domains`, `financial_panel_blocked_domains`, and `investor_financial_questions`. Fallback to older compact financial sections is allowed only if it does not reintroduce broad false warnings.

It must distinguish `usable_current_metrics`, `usable_derived_metrics`, `partial_metrics`, `unreliable_metrics`, `invalid_or_quarantined_metrics`, `precise_missing_metrics`, `trend_durability_limits`, `precision_limits`, `allowed_financial_warnings`, `blocked_financial_warnings`, `rewritten_financial_warnings`, `investor_financial_questions`, `financial_panel_status`, and `source_provenance`. Analysts must not emit any warning present in `blocked_financial_warnings`; they must instead use the corresponding precise or rewritten limitation.

Committee aggregation may form consensus only from allowed financial warnings and those explicit truth classes; blocked stale warnings must never become committee consensus.

If current usable financial truth shows FCF/owner-earnings, identified capex, or payables/payable-days are available, clean analyst artifacts, `committee_synthesis.json`, and `committee_brief.md` must rewrite or drop the stale missing-data wording and keep the original blocked wording only in diagnostics.

Saved `<analyst>_analysis.json` files may keep `status`, `validation_status`, `evidence_grounding_status`, `financial_truth_consistency_status`, clean warning lists, and user-safe summaries that blocked stale or internal raw financial warnings were handled, while raw blocked-warning text and internal-label details stay only in `<analyst>_analysis_diagnostics.json`.

Analysts may interpret only precomputed metrics already present in PCIM, must surface missing-data and comparability limits explicitly, and must not calculate new ratios in the LLM layer.

Deterministic aliases such as owner-earnings, identified-capex, capex-deployed, closing-shares, receivables/inventory/payables value labels, working-capital day labels, and conservative-FCF labels must normalize to canonical metric IDs before validation. The runner must parse raw JSON, build a deterministic analyst skeleton, merge draft content into that skeleton, repair malformed `assessment` / `financial_assessment` / `user_facing_brief` / list / enum fields, move unknown fields to diagnostics, then run financial-warning carry-forward, evidence normalization, and strict validation. `_validate_llm_panel_output` must never validate raw unnormalized LLM output directly.

LLM-provided brief prose may fill explanatory fields such as bullets, bottom line, and optional financial lens, but canonical title/lens mismatches must be overwritten and recorded in diagnostics rather than failing as raw LLM drift.

PCIM section-name aliases such as `risk_inputs`, `financial_quality_inputs`, `governance_and_incentive_inputs`, and other compact-section labels are not evidence IDs; they must be removed from every nested analyst evidence list before save, recorded under `evidence_id_normalization.removed_invalid_ids`, and leave the saved analyst output at `warning` rather than `fail` when the cleanup is hygiene-only. Unknown evidence IDs are likewise removed from active evidence lists but retained in diagnostics; a final recursive assertion must prevent invalid evidence IDs from being written.

Governance/incentive claims may not cite market-risk / FX / interest-rate evidence unless the claim is explicitly about risk oversight or risk governance; misrouted governance claims must be rerouted to governance/ownership/capital-allocation/corporate-action/uncertainty evidence when such evidence is available to Munger, or converted into limitation / open-uncertainty language before final grounding validation. Major financial warnings from selected PCIM context must be preserved semantically in analyst output via `financial_missing_data`, `financial_interpretation_limits`, or `financial_warnings_carried_forward`, with precise wording preferred over generic placeholders. The system must build deterministic required warning groups from selected financial PCIM context, auto-carry any omitted major warning before final validation, record that repair in `schema_warnings`, and still fail hard if the analyst contradicts the required warning (for example by claiming FCF, owner earnings, or FCF-backed dividend quality when FCF/capex is missing). In particular, share-count warnings must distinguish `share count missing` from `weighted average shares missing` / `diluted shares missing`, and generic missing-data claims must not be emitted when a more specific supported limitation is available.

After recursive compaction, each included PCIM section must still obey deterministic post-compaction char caps (`multi_year_inputs` 4000; `financial_quality_inputs`, `capital_allocation_inputs`, `management_quality_inputs`, `moat_inputs`, `governance_and_incentive_inputs` 3000; `business_understanding` and `business_economics_inputs` 2500; other sections 2500). `multi_year_inputs` must preserve `years_covered`, `limitations`, compact evidence traceability, and no `source_chunk`, while section-over-budget fallbacks must collapse to limitation objects rather than silently leaking oversized payloads into analyst prompts.
- Investor-panel prompt packing is now doctrine-aware, class-budgeted, and hard-gated on the full prompt rather than only broad section caps. ## 10. Next Milestone

### Investor-intelligence semantic boundary

- Every upstream company-memory candidate must carry actor/ownership, statement or stream classification, investor relevance, materiality, period basis, eligibility, evidence confidence, and semantic status before promotion.
- Management commitments accept company-owned commitment speech acts only. Government targets, industry forecasts, historical facts, existing capabilities, CSR activity, and unbounded aspirations remain quarantined.
- Projects, capacity, commentary, risks, and capital allocation must enforce their own economic boundaries. Structural validity cannot substitute for investor relevance.
- Management Quality is an upstream-only synthesis. It consumes commitments, projects, capacity, risks, commentary, capital allocation, and governed financial evidence; it must not consume Investor Panel or Committee conclusions.
- A synthesized artifact must not predate an upstream dependency it claims to consume. Stale lineage is a hard failure.
- Investor Panel, Committee, and Ask IntrinsicIQ may be regenerated only after all seven upstream semantic gates pass and have been manually audited.

### Production financial-output reuse and blocking contract

- The `production` profile may reuse an existing year-level fundamentals set only when every governed financial artifact is present, each artifact embeds the active company and year, validation and reconciliation are non-failing, and the year-level financial audit is `pass` or `warning` with no hard failures. Reuse is same-period only; values from another reporting year must never be substituted.
- Primary balance-sheet extraction may restore a generic `TOTAL` to `Total Assets` only at the explicit assets-to-`EQUITY AND LIABILITIES` boundary. Ordinary subtotals remain generic and unmapped.
- A failing year-level `financial_audit_report.json` is a blocking `financials` stage result. Production must stop before financial memory, panel, or customer-facing output rather than treating a completed but failed audit as success.
- Current shares outstanding may use an explicit end-of-period share-capital-note row, but opening/beginning share counts are not current-period substitutes. Monetary fundamentals accept only monetary-typed extracted values, and net worth may be transparently derived as sourced equity share capital plus sourced reserves with formula/provenance retained.
- Primary balance-sheet discovery is both heading-aware and structure-aware. A titleless/split-page statement may be recognized from period columns, table headers, assets, liabilities, and equity structure even when the statement title is on an adjacent page or trailing accounting-policy text shares the chunk. A usable balance sheet requires assets, equity/net worth, and liabilities; debt itself is optional. Total liabilities may be transparently derived as `total_assets - net_worth` when both inputs are sourced and the result is non-negative, but missing debt must remain missing rather than being fabricated.
- Structural statement classification must be mutually guarded: an explicit or structural operating-cash-flow signature takes precedence over the balance-sheet fallback because cash-flow working-capital and financing rows naturally mention assets, liabilities, and equity. Titleless cash-flow pages may be recognized from period columns, table headers, and operating-cash-flow rows.
- LLM input-pack budgets apply to the final serialized pack, including size metadata and truncation counters. Pack size metadata must be measured to a serialization fixed point. Business-intelligence compaction reserves bounded serialization headroom so metadata updates cannot push an otherwise compacted pack over its declared stage budget.

Regenerate stale committee synthesis artifacts against the new financial committee contract, then continue tightening investor-panel prompt compaction for real company PCIMs while keeping doctrine-declared financial coverage and the no-new-ratio rule intact.
