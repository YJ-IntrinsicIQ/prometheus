# Prometheus Intelligence Manifesto

Prometheus exists for one reason:
to turn raw company data into durable investor intelligence.

We do not want another screener.
We do not want a wall of numbers.
We do not want presentation that looks busy but explains little.

Prometheus must help an investor understand what changed, why it changed, and whether conviction should change because of it.

## The core belief

Intelligence comes before numbers.

Numbers matter, but numbers alone do not tell the investor what to think.
The product must answer the higher question:

What is the company becoming?

That means Prometheus must explain business motion, management intent, execution quality, and economic direction.

## The public answer contract

Every public conclusion should follow this shape:

Question  
→ Conclusion  
→ Progression  
→ Why it matters  
→ Evidence  
→ Raw numbers, when useful

This order matters.
It keeps the product grounded in judgment, not data dumping.

## What Prometheus must remember

Prometheus should not just store facts.
It should remember the company through time.

The memory layers of the product are:

- Projects
- Promises
- Risks
- Capacity
- Capital Allocation
- Initiatives
- Commentary

These are not decorative labels.
They are the structure of business memory.

If the product loses these layers, it loses its mind.

## Canonical evidence comes first

When Prometheus answers a question, it should prefer the strongest governed company-memory layer available before falling back to raw document evidence.

That means:

- commitments should come from the commitment layer
- execution should come from the progression layers
- financial interpretation should come from reconciled financial truth
- public answers should use raw document evidence only when the question truly requires it or the governed memory is still incomplete

This keeps the product from drifting back into a document reader.
It also keeps public answers stable, comparable, and easier to trust.

## Semantic guardrails come before promotion

Not every structurally valid item deserves to become company memory.

Before a candidate is promoted into a progression stream or synthesis layer, Prometheus should ask:

- Is this core investor evidence, or just context?
- Which period does it belong to?
- Is it material enough to change a progression judgment?

If the answer is unclear, historically distant, outside the analysis window, or not meaningfully about the business, the item should be quarantined instead of promoted. That rule protects the product from civic noise, stale history, and narrative drift.

## Progression matters more than snapshots

A single year is rarely the truth.
An investor needs the path, not only the point in time.

Prometheus must show:

- how a metric has evolved
- how management language has changed
- how evidence has accumulated or weakened
- how the latest state compares with the original commitment

The product must answer:

What changed?

Why did it change?

Should investor conviction change because of it?

## Teach while analyzing

Prometheus should educate while it analyzes.

If the user sees a ratio, the product should help them understand what it means.
If the user sees a trend, the product should explain why it matters.
If the user sees risk, the product should show the mechanism behind it.

The goal is not just interpretation.
The goal is investor understanding.

Charts, labels, callouts, and explanations should help the user learn the business.
They should never exist only to decorate the page.

## Investor lenses are evolving judgments

Prometheus must not treat investor thinking as a static dashboard.
An investor lens is a judgment that evolves as evidence evolves.

That means the system must remember:

- the original claim
- the later confirmation or contradiction
- the timing of the evidence
- whether the conviction should rise, fall, or stay unchanged

The product should not simply repeat the latest number.
It should show how a thesis has developed.

## Capital allocation must be remembered, not assumed

Capital allocation is not only about where money went.
It is about what happened afterward.

Prometheus must preserve the path from deployment to outcome:

- what was funded
- why it was funded
- what management expected
- what later evidence shows
- whether the result supports or weakens the original judgment

We do not infer success without evidence.
We do not declare delivery without proof.
We do not turn ambition into accomplishment too early.

If the evidence never arrives, the system must say so clearly.

## Evidence and uncertainty discipline

Prometheus must be confident only when the evidence supports confidence.

The product must distinguish:

- confirmed
- partially confirmed
- delayed
- contradicted
- unable to verify

Uncertainty is not weakness.
It is honesty.

The product should make uncertainty visible instead of hiding it behind polished language.

## Charts must explain change

Charts are useful when they reveal motion.
They are weak when they are only ornament.

Use charts to show:

- direction
- turning points
- acceleration or slowdown
- divergence between promise and delivery
- progression in conviction

Do not use charts to repeat what a single number already says.

## Failure is part of the intelligence system

Every failure in the pipeline is a learning event.

If a document breaks the system, Prometheus should remember:

- what failed
- where it failed
- why it failed
- whether the issue is local or general
- what test or validator should prevent it next time

The correct response to failure is not silence.
It is institutional memory.

Every meaningful bug should create one or more of these:

- a regression test
- a validator
- a fallback
- a quarantine rule
- a clearer contract

If the same failure can happen twice, the system has not learned enough.

## Never drift into a traditional screener

Prometheus must resist the easy collapse into a normal finance app.

If the product becomes a place where users only scan ratios, ranks, and isolated numbers, it has lost its purpose.

The product must always preserve its original promise:

to explain businesses, not merely list them.

To help investors understand execution, not just exposure.

To convert filings and commentary into memory, progression, and judgment.

## The standard of success

Prometheus is successful when an investor can answer these questions quickly and clearly:

- What did management commit to?
- What happened afterwards?
- Did execution match the commitment?
- What changed over time?
- What does the evidence really say?
- Should conviction change because of it?

If the product can answer those questions well, it is doing its job.

If it cannot, it is drifting.

## Closing principle

Prometheus should be an intelligence system first and a display layer second.

It should remember the company through time.
It should explain change.
It should teach while it analyzes.
It should respect uncertainty.
It should learn from failure.
It should protect investor judgment.

That is the product.
That is the standard.
That is the promise.
